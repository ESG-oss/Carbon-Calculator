Methodology and notes

  1. Factors. All figures use the UK Government GHG Conversion Factors for Company Reporting (DEFRA / DESNZ), 2026 set, expressed as kgCO₂e per litre of fuel.
  2. HVO. Under the GHG Protocol, the CO₂ from burning HVO is biogenic and reported outside the scopes, so the factor here covers only the trace CH₄ and N₂O. This is why HVO's Scope 1 figure is so much lower than diesel's.
  3. Well-to-tank. The WTT option adds Scope 3 upstream fuel and energy-related emissions — extraction, refining, processing and delivery — to the combustion factor.
  4. Prices. Any price per litre is entered by you. Cost figures are arithmetic on the figures you supply, exclude VAT, delivery and any rebates unless your entered price already includes them, and do not constitute a quotation from HSS ProService Marketplace.
  5. Status of results. These results are indicative and provided for guidance only. They do not constitute verified emissions assurance.
  6. Updates. DEFRA publishes a new factor set each year, typically in June. This tool is updated annually; the set in use is shown at the top of the page.
